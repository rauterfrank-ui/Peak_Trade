U04-as-source. This is `CURRENT_PRODUCTIVE_DESIGN`. The sealed legacy census
is not reopened. GET is evidence, not authority, and is not required to
define this producer.

typed current-productive fact and remains unbound. Venue `eq` remains
reconciliation-target only. Forbidden fields are not algebra inputs. U04
remains a sizing reservation reduction after BASE. P01 is a conditional
reduction. Unclassified venue fields remain NON_AUTHORITY.
