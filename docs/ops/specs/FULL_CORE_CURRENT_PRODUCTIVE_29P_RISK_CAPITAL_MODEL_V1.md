# Full Core Current Productive 29P Risk Capital Model V1

Derived spec. Non-SSOT. Canonical persist is Master Runbook §11.2.1.CU.
Consumes Owner-GO
`OWNER_GO_CURRENT_PRODUCTIVE_29P_RISK_CAPITAL_MODEL_REDESIGN_AND_VENUE_FIELD_AUTHORITY_ADJUDICATION_V1`.
Supersedes the CT-named BASE-binding GO. The sealed CT producer identity remains
the 29P source object. The sealed CS forbid of a direct venue-field 29P claim
remains. This persist is `CURRENT_PRODUCTIVE_DESIGN`. GET is evidence, not
authority, and is not required to adjudicate field semantics. No Hope-GET.

STEP-29P needs USDC cross-margin free margin available to size new risk.
Official venue semantics and the in-repo AVAILABLE_MARGIN contract identify
that quantity as `details[ccy=USDC].availEq`, already net of in-use including
open orders. BASE is retired as an unobservable intermediate. Algebra is
`details[ccy=USDC].availEq - P01_IF_APPLIES`. Direct 29P `source_field=availEq`
remains forbidden; the producer wraps the observation. Venue `eq` remains
reconciliation-target only.
