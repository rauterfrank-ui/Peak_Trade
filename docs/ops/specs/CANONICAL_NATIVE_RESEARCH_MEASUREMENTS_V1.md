## Regular experiment provider

NativeResearchExecutionContextV1 carries explicit existing-owner identity template,
measured champion, time horizon, universe, nonempty gap dimensions, threshold
policy and robustness policy; optional stores remain existing-owner contracts.
NativeResearchExperimentProviderV1 explicitly resolves a Search-requested native
region over the identity template; this is distinct from the measurement function,
which still requires fully resolved parameters. It checks candidate identity,
champion receipt identity/content digest, exact bar horizon and configured base
parameters. The Search hypothesis fingerprint must match the actual robustness
policy before any measurement. Both comparison scores are measured full-run
nonannual pre-funding engine Sharpe. No fixed fixture score or ranking substitute.

The observer receives detached snapshots. The verified robustness policy is also
snapshotted for the returned request; callbacks cannot rewrite that policy after
its fingerprint check. Existing Phase10 owns persistence and further decisions.
No missing Shadow/gap values are manufactured. Missing inputs remain rejected or
BLOCKED by their existing owners. [25-test connected provider and GVEF reload proof](../evidence/native_research_regular_provider_closure_v1_2026_11_04/README.md).

Funding research estimates must remain distinct from observed accounting events.
The signal-versus-held-quantity counterexample does not authorize a research-to-
productive-accounting bypass, a second cash writer or invented settlement ordering.
