## Bound outcome

CASE_B remains historically true: no unique owner or hook existed before
this slice. This slice mints
`SECTION_11_14_LIVE_PRODUCTIVE_CAPTURE_OWNER_V1` as the unique runtime
owner of `BOUND_FILL_PROVEN ∧ RESTART_NOT_YET_OCCURRED`. The storage
owner remains the storage owner. The unique hook
`run_capture_hook_after_bound_fill_before_restart_v1` is the sole
productive caller of `commit_handoff_after_bound_fill_before_restart_v1`.
The producer remains internal to the writer. Identity is parameterized
from the future bound fill. Qty is Peak_Trade-owned S05 and must
Decimal-equal `fillSz`. The hook is structurally reachable on
`LIVE_ORDER` and currently unauthorized. Offline remains authorized and
cannot reach the hook. No GET. No POST. No restart execution. No
productive handoff write during this persist. Host-crash durability
remains `UNPROVEN`. `LIVE_RESTART_RECONSTRUCTED` remains false.
`CONTEMPORANEOUS_PEAK_TRADE_PRE_RESTART_HANDOFF_OBSERVED` remains false.
