# E5: executed evaluator fanout, exact result receipts and quality boundaries

Date: 2026-11-05. P13 and AC31–AC33. Authority remains NONE.
This is an explicit offline replay-contract profile, not full domain/economic
certification or the combined E6 system episode.

## Original purpose and actual baseline

GVEF's retained V1.5 source excerpts and the Runbook's 2026-11-04 relationship /
original-design assessment establish its external, non-authorizing evaluation role.
The five existing evaluator classes are Ranking Universe, Productive Trading Path,
Self-Learning, Optimization Universe and Market Intelligence. These are not five
N5 lanes. Historical protected digest domains, EvidenceBundleV1 and existing
failure/reproof routing remain unchanged.

The original `whole_system_runner_v1()` sets an integrated failure classification;
it does not invoke all evaluators. The existing runner consumes one supplied
DomainEvaluationContext at a time. Its retained protected-output projection omits
candidate result content and complete corpus/numeric artifacts. Earlier P12 fixes
protect repeatability and object aliases but do not expand those historical hash
domains. The new E5 binding is additive and does not retroactively redefine an old
bundle, tag or passing fixture as full domain-quality proof.

## Explicit plan and actual execution

EvaluationPlanV1 binds a change, affected consumer edges, source-tree generation
and every one of the five domain dispositions. Each domain must have either a
supported required contract or a NOT_REQUIRED impact reason plus consumer edges.
Missing required input yields INCOMPLETE. An unsupported, failed, incomplete or
substituted implementation yields BLOCKED. A WHOLE_SYSTEM tag never supplies
missing execution or requirement coverage.

The selected requirement scopes are intentionally narrow:

- RU_MEMBERSHIP_AND_SELECTION_BOUNDARY: original membership/digest results and
  `ru.not_selection`; no claim of economically optimal ranking or Scope math.
- PTP_ORDER_AND_PRE_EXTERNAL_BOUNDARY: original stage order/owner/terminal checks;
  supplied trace validation is not a newly executed venue/Core trading episode.
- SL_ARTIFACT_AND_NO_WRITEBACK: original artifact digest and no-writeback checks,
  plus a retained complete native research packet. Not a model-quality claim.
- OPT_PARAMETER_AND_NO_APPLY: original parameter count/delta and no productive
  apply. Not an optimizer-quality or search-profitability claim.
- MI_CONTEXT_AND_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY: original context/authority/leakage flags and
  exact original MI numeric evidence where bound. Flags alone do not establish
  natural causality, complete source authentication or market freshness.

The coordinator admits exact existing evaluator classes and actual canonical
ConstraintMatrix pre/post gates, VectorCorpusIntegrityGate, protected comparator
and original GVEF registry. Pass-through corpus gates, fixture bundle builders,
unknown substitutes and different evaluator IDs are not certified. Comparator
expected-change policy, gate roots, evaluator version and implementation files
are pinned along with the full context. These are cooperative code/profile checks,
not a sandbox against arbitrary malicious Python or concurrent external writers.

A new context-bound evidence builder copies actual context and candidate
invariants into the unchanged V1 bundle and uses the original V1 semantic digest.
The original runner is invoked separately for each required domain. A wrapper
retains the actual returned baseline/candidate calls; successful execution requires
both repetitions, matching complete results, concrete invariant/metric coverage,
actual registry readback and unchanged bound corpus after execution.

## Corpus, results and numeric retention

Each selected invocation has one explicit vector. Its additive
E5_EXPLICIT_REPLAY_SNAPSHOT_V1 selector binds the complete supplied ReplayTrace
content digest, exact invariant set and declared-replay-contract disposition.
The original corpus registry validates its manifest/vector identity. Original
corpora are not rewritten or relabeled; the acceptance fixtures use separate
explicit temporary corpora. Broader corpus populations require broader actual
cases, not extra unsupported invariant labels in this profile.

DomainResultBinding retains complete context, corpus/vector snapshots, actual
call results, run record, comparator/delta, bundle, dependency binding and exact
numeric references. EvaluationReceiptV1 binds both the historical bundle digest
and a separate complete-result digest. SystemEvaluationReceipt binds the explicit
plan and every required domain receipt; successful registration and durable
archiving remain distinct states.

NativeResearchExecutionArchiveV1 and MiOfflineDurableEvidenceStoreV1 stay the
owners of their original numeric records. Their original readers validate inputs
before retention. E5 copies complete original packet bytes losslessly, with their
own byte SHA, and never quantizes floats to fit float-free GVEF V1. Reload validates
native full measurement / information and MI forecast/calibration/research triad
through the original validators. The original native receipt retains four engine
runs; the MI scalar and forecast error remain distinct actual numbers.

The new immutable archive uses content-addressed receipts and lossless numeric
bytes. A cooperative archive lock serializes publication. Completed domains can
be reused while a missing domain later executes; old incomplete receipts remain
immutable. Readback reconstructs corpus validation in memory and needs neither a
live evaluator invocation nor the original temporary corpus/MI owner location.
It validates full results and numbers rather than trusting old ProtectedDigest.
A historical receipt cannot certify a new source generation: the expected current
plan remains an explicit input. No automatic migration or archive GC is provided.

## Economic quality and remaining boundaries

The current profile has no ratified positive economic-quality contract or complete
independent natural comparison owner. Its economic consumer therefore stays
BLOCKED, identifying missing independent comparison/data, costs, funding, horizon,
uncertainty and target metric. Even caller-supplied complete flags cannot turn those
claims into validated evidence or remove the unratified-contract blocker.

This deliberately implements the required boundary, not an unsupported positive
quality evaluator. Mechanical contract PASS can coexist with economic BLOCKED.
E4's policy-driven change of measured candidate does not establish profitability,
natural usefulness or improvement; its lower selected full score remains evidence.
A future positive quality claim requires independently reviewable input owners,
metric/cost/funding semantics and uncertainty policy, then a separately supported
quality consumer. No missing market/account observations are invented in E5.

No original evaluator, V1 digest domain, corpus/config, Scope formula/parameter,
financial owner or historical ledger is changed by this additive package. There is
no promotion handoff, training, productive apply, network, live/testnet, credential,
order, capital movement or operational authorization. E6 combined offline system
acceptance and all broader source/economic claims remain separate.

The join is validated separately from validity of each numeric record: every
Self-Learning replay artifact must equal its original retained native packet's
GVEF reference, and every MI replay bundle must name its retained calibration
packet exactly. Two independently valid but unrelated records do not satisfy
that binding. These joins are checked before evaluation and again on readback.
