evidence. U04 KIND_SET disposition remains the sealed AR closeout
`NOT_EQUITY_STOCK_AFFECTING` and is not reclassified. U04 algebra inclusion
remains `UNRESOLVED`. Venue GET predicates are evaluated and unsatisfied.
KIND_SET remains `EMPTY_FAIL_CLOSED`. Mapping remains not canonically valid.
