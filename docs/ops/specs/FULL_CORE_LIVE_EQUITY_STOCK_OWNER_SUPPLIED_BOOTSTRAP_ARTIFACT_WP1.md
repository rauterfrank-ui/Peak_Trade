This layer consumes sealed BQ acquisition persist and reuses
BOOTSTRAP_STOCK_ACQUISITION_V1. It defines the owner-supplied ingest
boundary: raw bytes are preserved, SHA-256 identified, and validated
without implicit normalization. A schema-valid fixture candidate is not
the canonical stock anchor and does not become a KIND_SET member.
Ratification requires a separate canonical authority that this GO does
not grant. Reconstruction, checkpoint, classified event-stream, and
venue `eq` bindings remain unbound until a ratified anchor exists. Venue
`eq` remains witness/reconciliation-target only. Checkpoint cannot mint
equity. AVAILABLE_FOR_SIZING and P01 remain after and separate from
equity. Today no owner-supplied forensic artifact is present.
